# Beta Manual Testing Tracker

## Goal

Use EngineeringOS daily to learn, become interview-ready, and get a job.

## Week 1 Checklist

- [ ] Day 1: Complete onboarding and start the saved role path.
- [ ] Day 2: Complete one DSA or algorithm lesson and one practice problem.
- [ ] Day 3: Complete one Node/DB/backend lesson and one explain-back response.
- [ ] Day 4: Complete one HLD/AWS lesson and one design answer.
- [ ] Day 5: Complete one LLD or Staff/EM lesson and one interview response.
- [ ] Day 6: Run a timed mock interview session.
- [ ] Day 7: Review weak areas, readiness score, and career assets.

## Feedback Log

Capture after each session:

- What was confusing?
- What felt shallow?
- What helped interview readiness?
- What should be automated?
- What blocked daily continuation?

## Exit Criteria

- The next lesson is always clear.
- Practice maps to interview questions.
- Feedback improves the next answer.
- Weak areas become visible.
- The product makes job preparation easier than scattered resources.

# Phase 69: Information Architecture Simplification

## Goal

Reduce cognitive overload across Dashboard, Courses, Syllabus, and Learning Graph by making each page answer one primary question and moving dense secondary material behind progressive disclosure.

## Page Jobs

- Dashboard: What should I do now?
- Courses: What path am I on, and what paths exist?
- Course detail: What stage/topic should I do next?
- Syllabus: How do I search or browse a specific topic?
- Learning Graph: Where am I in the roadmap visually?
- Progress: Where are the detailed readiness and analytics signals?

## Implementation Scope

- Dashboard: keep Resume Course and Today Mission prominent; collapse analytics/readiness/product metrics.
- Courses: remove duplicated stage detail wall from the catalog page; keep active roadmap plus course cards.
- Syllabus: show recommended topics first; keep full topic catalog collapsed unless searching/filtering.
- Learning Graph: show saved target course first; put adjacent paths behind an expandable section.
- Preserve all content and routes; reduce first-screen density.

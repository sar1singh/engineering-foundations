Design a Global CDN Caching Layer

High Availability Eventual Consistency Read-Heavy

Scenario Context

You are the lead architect for a rapidly growing video streaming platform. The platform serves millions of users globally, delivering both static assets (thumbnails, metadata) and dynamic video chunks. Recently, origin servers have been buckling under load during high-traffic events (e.g., live sports, new season releases).

Your task is to design a geographically distributed caching layer that sits between the users and the origin servers to drastically reduce origin load and improve edge latency.

Core Requirements

  • Scale: Handle 10M+ concurrent users globally.
  • Latency: Target P99 latency < 50ms for cache hits.
  • Durability: Cache nodes may fail; the system must gracefully degrade and recover without overwhelming the origin.
  • Invalidation: Need a mechanism to propagate updates to cached metadata within 60 seconds globally.

Specific Challenges to Address

"How do you prevent a 'thundering herd' when a highly anticipated piece of content is published, but the cache is cold across all PoPs (Points of Presence)?"

Evaluation Rubric

0/5

Self-assess your architectural design against these critical criteria before advancing.
